About Us
BORN IN BOBCAT COUNTRY

GoBoko was founded by a group of Texas State University students who were tired of the same old energy drinks that left them crashing before their next class. We wanted something better — something made for the student grind.

Named after the Bobcat battle cry, GoBoko is more than a brand. It's a rallying call for every student pulling late nights at Alkek, grinding through group projects, and showing up on game day. Our products are crafted to deliver clean, sustained energy without the sugar crash.

Based right here in San Marcos, Texas, we're proud to be built by Bobcats, for Bobcats — and for anyone who needs that extra push to get through the day.
